Replace the demo notifications in StoneRate’s transporter notification drawer with real notifications for these events only:

1. NEW BIDDING OPPORTUNITY
Notify the transporter when a new bidding opportunity becomes available to that transporter.

2. ORDER ASSIGNED
Notify the transporter when an order is assigned:
- Through direct assignment.
- Through winning a bid.

Clearly identify the assignment method and relevant order.

3. TRANSPORTER’S OWN MATERIAL-REQUEST UPDATES
For material orders requested by the transporter from sellers, notify the transporter when:
- A seller accepts the request or a material within it.
- A material or request is fully cancelled.
- A material quantity is partially cancelled or reduced.

For cancellations, identify the affected material, cancelled quantity, remaining quantity, recorded unit, and reason where available. Distinguish material-level changes from whole-request changes.

DISPLAY REQUIREMENTS
- Show the latest 60 notifications for the logged-in transporter, newest first.
- Use real events only. Remove demo notifications.
- Do not show another transporter’s notifications.
- Avoid duplicate notifications for the same event.
- Preserve read/unread state.
- Clicking a notification should open the relevant bid, order, or material request.
- Keep the existing notification drawer design.
- Do not add payment, reminder, or other notification types.

CHANGE BOUNDARIES
Make only the changes necessary to connect these notifications. Do not unnecessarily modify existing APIs, login/OTP, order processing, bidding rules, assignments, cancellation logic, or unrelated workflows.
