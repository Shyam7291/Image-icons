Create a new responsive StoneRate Admin page named:

StoneRateAdminBuyerVerification.js

PAGE PURPOSE

This page allows an authorized StoneRate Admin to review and decide a Buyer’s business-verification submission.

The Buyer will submit:

1. Aadhaar verification status from the approved Aadhaar verification provider
2. A live photograph of the shop frontage or hoarding
3. A live selfie captured in front of the same shop hoarding
4. Coordinates captured during the shop photograph
5. Coordinates captured during the selfie
6. Location accuracy in metres
7. Capture timestamps
8. Registered Buyer and shop information

The Admin uses this information to assess whether:

- The Buyer’s identity has been verified
- The submitted business appears connected to the Buyer
- The shop name or hoarding is visible
- The two photographs appear to be taken at the same business location
- The captured location is consistent with the registered business location
- A new submission is required
- The Buyer can be approved for commercial activity

IMPORTANT TERMINOLOGY

Keep these verification concepts separate:

AADHAAR VERIFICATION

Confirms the Buyer’s identity through the authorized Aadhaar provider.

BUSINESS VERIFICATION

Confirms that the Buyer has submitted adequate evidence of a construction-material shop or relevant commercial establishment.

ACCOUNT STATUS

Controls whether the account is:

- Active
- Restricted
- Blocked
- Deleted

Do not use one field for all three concepts.

BUSINESS VERIFICATION ACCESS RULE

Buyer account status and business-verification status must remain separate.

Example:

Active

Business Verification
Under Review

An Active but unverified Buyer can:

- Sign in
- View profile
- View previous records
- Contact Support
- Submit verification

An Active but unverified Buyer cannot:

- Create a new Rate Request
- Accept a quotation
- Create a new commercial order

A Verified Buyer can perform normal Buyer commercial actions.

A Restricted account remains restricted even if business verification is approved.

A Blocked account cannot sign in.

selfie

VERIFY BUYER

  note: "Submitted evidence reviewed and approved.",
  checklist: {
    shopVisible: true,
    shopNameReadable: true,
    shopNameConsistent: true,
    selfieWithShopClear: true,
    locationReviewed: true
  }
}

REQUEST RESUBMISSION

POST /api/admin/buyer-verifications/:buyerPublicId/request-resubmission

Body:

{
  reasons: [
    "shop_name_not_readable",
    "selfie_background_unclear"
  ],
  replace: [
    "shop_photo",
    "selfie_photo"
  ],
  note: "Please upload photographs with the complete hoarding visible."
}

REJECT VERIFICATION

POST /api/admin/buyer-verifications/:buyerPublicId/reject

{
  reasons: [
    "business_information_inconsistent"
  ],

}
