- No bids

- Urgent first
- Most applications
- Lowest bid
- Highest distance

1. Newest first:

2. Urgent first:
   Show live biddings with the least remaining time first.

3. Most applications:
   Show biddings with the highest number of Transporter applications first.

4. Lowest bid:
   Show the bidding with the lowest current bid first.

   Show the longest routes first.

The Urgent filter should show live biddings with very little time remaining.
